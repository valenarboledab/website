Teleférico Fátima: Structural BIM for a Cable Car

Location
Colombia
Type
Infrastructure · Cable car
My role
Structural drawings · Structural BIM · Coordination meetings · Site visits

A cable car is public infrastructure at the scale of the city. On Teleférico Fátima I worked on the structural side of the project, from the drawings to the site.

My role

I was responsible for the structural drawing work, managed the structural BIM model, took part in the project coordination meetings and followed the work on site.

“Good coordination is what lets a complex structure become reality.”

Why it matters

Infrastructure like this changes how people move through the city every day, and it depends on precise documentation and close coordination between design and construction.
