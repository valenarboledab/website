Arboretum 2: Architecture That Takes In the Mountains

Location
Colombia
Type
Commercial
My role
Full architectural design · 3D renders
Recognition
Winning project

Arboretum 2 is a commercial project in Colombia built around one idea: the architecture should absorb the mountains around it, not stand against them.

The idea

The design takes in the shape of the mountain landscape. Instead of rigid blocks, the complex is organized as flowing, continuous forms that follow the terrain and frame the views.

Architecture and nature together

Nature is part of the program, not a decoration around it. Planted terraces, gardens and open plazas run through the whole complex, so moving through it feels like walking through a landscape.

“When architecture follows the land, nature becomes part of every space.”

My role

I developed the complete architectural design of the project and produced its 3D renders.

Recognition

Arboretum 2 was a winning project.
